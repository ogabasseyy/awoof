School-account control and current student status are different. Your benefits depend on the second.

01 / CONNECT

Your school account.

Start with your school details. Proving access to a school account is the first step, not the final student-status decision.

02 / VERIFY

Your student status.

Current enrollment needs an approved evidence source. When that connection is unavailable, your status stays pending.

With eligibility confirmed, participating merchants can apply their student benefit. Availability and offer terms still apply.
