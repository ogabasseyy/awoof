How verification works — and its limits

Design prototype — synthetic content. Steps below are explanatory samples, not a live check.

  1. 1. Account sign-in

    Signing in proves control of an Awoof account. It does not by itself prove student status.

  2. 2. School-account proof

    Proving control of a school mailbox (for example a one-time code) shows mailbox control. It is not, on its own, proof of current enrollment.

  3. 3. Enrollment eligibility

    Whether you count as currently enrolled depends on current evidence and each merchant’s or institution’s policy — not on sign-in or a code alone.

Honest states you may see

  • Pending: proof submitted, decision not yet made. Wait for the stated next step.
  • Expired: an earlier proof is out of date. Re-check with current evidence.
  • Unavailable: some schools, checks, or benefits are not yet supported. The page will say so rather than guess.

Sample wording. This prototype performs no checks and stores nothing.
