01

Verification, step by step

Three checks. Three different answers.

Student verification fails when these get blurred together. Awoof keeps them apart so a mailbox code is never shown as proof of enrollment.

Account login

You sign in with your own credentials. This opens your account and nothing else.

Demonstration

School-account proof

A one-time code goes to a school mailbox. It confirms control of that mailbox on that day.

Demonstration

Enrollment eligibility

Current enrollment needs authoritative evidence from a connected source. Without one, the status stays pending — never “verified”.

Pending in demo

When a check cannot complete

  • Code never arrives — retry guidance and mailbox checks, not a dead end. See Help.
  • School not listed — an honest “not connected” state with what to do next, never a fake upload button.
  • Evidence expires — the status changes visibly; old proof is not shown as current.

Not available in this prototype: single sign-on, automated enrollment feeds, and manual document review. Any future availability will be labelled with its backend dependency and activation date.

03

For businesses

Offer student benefits without guessing who is a student.

Awoof asserts eligibility; your business applies the benefit. Each stage below would be labelled available or planned on the production page.

  1. Create a business account. Separate from student accounts from the start.
  2. Configure your offer. What the benefit is, and when it applies.
  3. Student consents to verification. Consent is explicit and can be withdrawn.
  4. You apply the benefit. Awoof’s answer informs your checkout; it does not run it.

Prototype honesty: integration stages, reporting, pricing, and timelines are not stated here because none is confirmed. No case studies or partner logos appear until real, approved ones exist.

For universities

Cooperation, described without exaggeration.

There is no list of partner institutions here, because there is no approved list to show. If your institution ever connects an enrollment source, this section would name exactly what evidence flows and what Awoof never claims — for example, a staff email address is not student enrollment.

  • What cooperation means: an authoritative enrollment source, with scope agreed in writing.
  • What is required: evidence Awoof can re-check, plus a way to handle withdrawals and expiry.
  • What is not claimed: partnerships, logos, or integrations that do not exist.

No university contact is listed because no monitored channel has been confirmed. Publishing an inbox or phone number here is a release blocker, not a detail.

Trust & limits

Plain answers, including “we don’t know yet”.

Is my data sold?
This prototype makes no data-handling claims. Production trust copy must cite real code, config, and tests — not intentions.
Is Awoof certified or audited?
No certifications, audits, uptime figures, or compliance statements appear here. None will ship without dated evidence in an internal claim register.
Where is the status page?
Nowhere yet. A status page needs live monitored data; a hardcoded green light would be dishonest, so there isn’t one.

06

Help

Failure states, answered first.

My verification code never arrived

Check the exact mailbox, wait a short time, then request a fresh code. If codes never arrive, your school’s mail system may be blocking them — the production help page will name the real next step.

My school isn’t listed

Not every school is connected. The honest status is “not connected”, with retry guidance — not a form that goes nowhere.

My status says pending or expired

Pending means evidence is missing or still being checked. Expired means old proof no longer counts. Neither is shown as “verified”.

How do I withdraw consent?

Consent withdrawal ends eligibility checks based on that consent. The production page will give the exact steps and what changes afterwards.
