## 1. Executive Summary & Core Legal Determinations

1. **Upstream NUC Licences:**
   - The primary documentation for the NUC CCMAS standards (specifically referenced in *Arts CCMAS 2023*, Page 2/copyright page) specifies the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Intergovernmental Organisation (CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO)** licence for textual material.
   - Other discipline documents (N01, N02, N03, N04, N06–N17) were published contemporaneously under the NUC Strategy Advisory Committee (STRADVCOM) curriculum modernization initiative.
2. **Distinction Between Facts vs. Creative Expressions:**
   - Under Nigerian Copyright Act 2022 § 2(2) and international copyright principles (TRIPS Art. 9.2; Berne Convention Art. 2(8)), **copyright does not extend to mere facts, ideas, procedures, systems, or mathematical concepts**.
   - Programme names (e.g., "B.A. Philosophy", "B.Sc. Computer Science") and official degree durations (e.g., 4 years for UTME, 3 years for DE 200) are uncopyrightable factual metadata.
   - However, curriculum descriptions, learning outcomes, syllabus text, and creative instructional guidance are protected expressive works.
3. **Compilation Rights & The Upstream Workbook:**
   - The intermediate workbook `NUC_Programme_Durations_2026-10-02_v2.xlsx` is an independent, non-official compilation prepared by a third party.
   - The workbook compiler added selection, coordination, and arrangement (e.g., evidence codes `G`, `P/G`, `P`, `C`, `V`, `P/R`, `P/U`, `L`, exception notes, and confidence flags).
   - The workbook itself contains **no explicit grant of licence, attribution statement, or public redistribution waiver**.
4. **Platform Safeguard Invariant:**
   - Because compiler authorization has not been executed and row-level duration correctness reviews remain pending, **all 237 rows in `nuc-v2.normalized.json` remain blocked**.
   - The platform never makes unverified estimate calculations, and graduation estimates return `unavailable` (`unreviewed_rule`).

## 3. Analysis of CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO Legal Obligations

The Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 IGO licence imposes four key operational obligations:

1. **Attribution (BY):**
   - Must provide credit to the National Universities Commission (NUC).
   - Must specify the title and URI of the source document.
   - Must retain copyright and licence notices.
2. **ShareAlike (SA):**
   - If an "Adaptation" (derivative work based upon the licensed work) is distributed publicly, that adaptation must be licensed under the same licence (CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO) or an approved compatible licence.
3. **No Endorsement:**
   - The licensee may not assert or imply any sponsorship, endorsement, or official certification by the NUC or the Federal Government of Nigeria.
4. **Third-Party Exclusions & Institutional Autonomy (The 30% Caveat):**
   - NUC CCMAS mandates 70% core curriculum, leaving 30% to individual university senates. University-specific variations, admission exceptions, and five-year transition programmes are governed by individual institutions, not the general CCMAS PDF.

## 4. Separation of Data vs. Code: Why Awoof Does NOT Open-Source Its Codebase

A critical software IP question is whether incorporating CC BY-SA reference datasets would "infect" the proprietary application code under ShareAlike.

### The Legal Boundaries:
1. **Facts are Not Copyrightable Works:**
   - Extracting raw programme names and standard nominal durations (e.g., `Mechanical Engineering: 5 years`) does not create a derivative work of the NUC text under copyright law. Facts cannot be copyrighted.
2. **Collective Works vs. Derivative Code:**
   - Under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO § 1(a), a work consisting of the licensed work alongside separate independent works in an assemblage is a "Collection" rather than an "Adaptation".
   - The application code (Next.js frontend, Express backend, PostgreSQL database schemas) is an independent creation that functions as a database software engine. Querying a factual database does not make the database engine a derivative work of the data.
3. **Isolation Architecture in Awoof:**
   - Reference datasets are stored in static JSON artifacts (`nuc-v2.normalized.json`).
   - The software communicates through decoupled service interfaces (`AcademicProgrammeReference` interface).
   - If reference data is ever published under CC BY-SA, the JSON data file and attribution documentation can be made available under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO without altering the proprietary commercial licensing of Awoof's platform application code.
